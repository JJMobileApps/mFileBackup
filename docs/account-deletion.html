mFileBackup Account and Data Deletion

App: mFileBackup
Developer: JJMobileApps
Last updated: November 2, 2026

mFileBackup is a self-hosted server and client application. Users operate their own server and choose where their accounts, backup files, and related data are stored. This page explains how to remove that information or request removal from the person who operates your server.

JJMobileApps does not operate your mFileBackup server and does not have access to, or authority to delete, its accounts or data. Account and data deletion is managed by the server owner or administrator. This page does not submit a deletion request or delete any data.

If you operate your own server

  1. On the device running the mFileBackup server, open the server's user management screen.
  2. Select the user account you want to remove, choose Delete, and confirm. This removes the user account record; it does not automatically erase all backup files or related server data.
  3. Separately review the account's storage folder and delete the backup files you want removed using the available file management tools. Check the location before deleting anything, especially if a folder contains data shared with other users.
  4. Review and remove any remaining related data you control, including gallery or backup metadata, thumbnails, device approval records, logs, trash, and additional backup copies, as applicable to your setup. Account deletion alone does not guarantee removal of these items.
  5. Stop or remove the relevant backup profiles on your client devices if you no longer want those devices to attempt backups. Review any files or settings retained on those devices separately.

If you have uninstalled the client app, you can still manage deletion directly on the server device or through the server's available administration and storage tools. Deleting the app from a client device does not delete information stored on your server.

If someone else operates your server

Contact the person who gave you the server address and login credentials, using the contact method you already use with that person. Ask them to delete your mFileBackup account and the associated data you want removed. You do not need to reinstall the client app to contact them.

Include your username and enough information to identify the server, and specify whether your request includes backup files, metadata, device approval records, and other copies. Do not send your password. Ask the administrator to confirm what was deleted, what remains, and when any retained information will be removed.

JJMobileApps does not maintain a directory of independently operated servers or their administrators and cannot identify or contact your administrator, process deletion on their behalf, or recover access to your server.

Deletion scope and retained data

Removing a user account is separate from deleting the files and other information associated with it. Disabling an account or revoking access is not the same as deleting the account and its data.

Data may remain in shared folders, logs, trash, storage snapshots, exports, or separate backups until the server owner removes it or the owner's retention settings expire. Original files on client devices are separate copies and must be managed on those devices.

The server owner decides the deletion scope and retention settings and is responsible for explaining any retained data, the reason for retention, and its retention period. JJMobileApps does not set or enforce a retention period for user-operated servers.

Processing time

For your own server, deletion takes place when you perform the relevant actions, subject to your storage and backup configuration. For a server operated by someone else, contact its administrator for the processing time and confirmation. JJMobileApps does not process or monitor these requests.
